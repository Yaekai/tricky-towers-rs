# Real game vs rebuild: differences seen in Max's screenshots (2026-10-06)

Max's screenshots of the original and of the rebuild at 2560x1440 (kept locally in
`research\screenshotsbymax`, not in the repo). Each line is a difference to fix; ✓ when done.

## Menus (all screens)
- ✓ Menu text is dark navy, bold, all caps in the original (row labels, buttons, titles); ours is
  pale/white mixed case and hard to read on the light panels.
- ✓ The button hints say SELECT (not CONTINUE) on Settings, Single Player and Trials, START GAME on
  Trials and Endless, plus a SHOP hint bottom-left on the title and Select Players. Ours draw
  CONTINUE/BACK partly off the right edge at 2560x1440.
- ✓ Page arrows point sideways (◀ ▶) and sit beside the panel; ours point up (▲).
- ✓ (labels, Help's ?, EXIT GAME) Title tiles: every tile has its name above it (LOCAL BATTLE, ONLINE BATTLE, SINGLE PLAYER,
  SHOP, HELP with a "?", SETTINGS, EXIT GAME); ours only label the picked tile, and the tiles sit
  larger and further right.
- ✓ Settings: sliders are a row of ten segments; Quality's top level is "Magical"; Language shows a
  flag; Apply is a small centred button, not a full row.
- (✓ carousel with neighbours and arrows, Left/Right picks the wizard; pack card under it, red ribbon on the picked button, Indie game logos) Single Player: a wizard carousel (three wizards, arrows, the pack's game logo for Indie
  wizards) with the brick pack below, then TRIALS and ENDLESS buttons (picked one is a red
  ribbon); ours shows one seat.
- ✓ Trials: title "ROOKIE TRIALS" (tier name + Trials); MEDALS: n/50 panel at the top right; only
  the next trial to play shows the wizard; ours shows a wizard on every button, the title is just
  the tier name, and the medal panel is cut off.
- ✓ Locked tier: LOCKED in pink above the chain, "EARN 8 TO UNLOCK" with the medal, arrows beside.
- (✓ Race, Puzzle, Survival order and RACE/PUZZLE/SURVIVAL CHALLENGE labels; no help line; still to do: the WEEKLY panel look, corners on the picked button, background per mode) Endless: WEEKLY leaderboard panel (rank, avatar, name, score, YOUR BEST row) on the left and
  three challenge buttons (RACE, PUZZLE, SURVIVAL) on the right, background per picked mode.
- ✓ Pause: CONTROLS panel on the left (Move, Rotate, Nudge, Light and Dark magic keys), GAME PAUSED
  big in the centre with RESUME (yellow, picked), RETRY and QUIT (pink) under it; the score and
  personal best stay visible dimmed.

- ✓ Select Players: with no pad, empty seats say CONNECT CONTROLLER (outlined) with no wizard or pack panel; no name plates or help line.

## Matches and trials
- ✓ No 1ST/2ND place labels in trials or Endless (ours shows them, sometimes twice).
- ✓ Trials frame both lanes: the player's tower on the left and the rival's lane on the right, with
  the enemy wizard beside the rival lane (not the player's).
- Race trials (✓ clock format and gold; all 13 race trials count down 44-155 s and fail at 0, matching the original): the finish line runs across both lanes; the clock reads ⌛0:58:44 (m:ss:cc) in
  gold, centred under the line; ours shows whole seconds by the player's lane.
- ✓ A failed trial says FAILED (pink) and a slanted panel bottom-right offers RETRY (yellow) and
  SELECT TRIAL (pink).
- (✓ count left of the card, gold outlined) Survival trials and Endless Survival: the brick count sits left of the next-brick card in gold;
  hearts under the wizard; the enemy wizard throws the cursed bricks from its lane.
- (✓ orb on its pillar; the enemy's brick column is still to do) Puzzle trials: the laser ends in a glowing orb at the player's lane; a pink beam on the
  enemy's side shows the bricks queued.
