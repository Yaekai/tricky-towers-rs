## New batch

- Pause keeps the match in Playing or Intro and pauses virtual time before Bevy's
  time update. Physics, CPU animation and shader time freeze together; HUD and
  spell clouds remain in the scene.
- Pause fades and the existing screen-clear fade use real time. Gameplay input
  stays blocked through fade-out. SFX/music restore and the cursor hides when
  closing begins; the existing Large-brick rumble follows that open/closing state.
- Game Info cannot open through pause. Brick-control sounds are suppressed while
  paused, and pause navigation retains its own selection sounds.
- Scripts use real time; Tap/Picture/Quit keep running during pause while gameplay
  commands wait for a fixed step. Timed offscreen shots and exit also use real
  time. The debug `--menu pause` entry builds its match before freezing.
- The cup winner emits its own appearance poof on the popup camera's layer, at
  its spawn position and pixel scale. Its rig starts at that position immediately.
- CastsPlugin initializes its required camera-shake resource itself, so headless
  consumers do not need access to the private effects type.
